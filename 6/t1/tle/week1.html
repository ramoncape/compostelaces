Lesson Proper

Fishery Arts Defined

Fishery Arts is a field under Technology and Livelihood Education (TLE) that deals with the raising, catching, processing, preserving, marketing, and management of fish and other aquatic resources.

Fishery Arts is one of the most important livelihood skills in the Philippines — especially here in the Visayas, where fishing communities depend on aquatic resources every day.

🌿 Key Terms to Know

Aquatic resourcesLiving things found in water — fish, seaweed, crabs, shrimp, etc.
LivelihoodA way of earning money to support a family
SustainableUsing resources in a way that does not run out or harm nature

Branch 1

🐟 Aquaculture

Aquaculture is the raising of fish, shellfish, and other aquatic organisms in controlled environments such as ponds, cages, tanks, or fish pens.

Unlike catching wild fish, aquaculture gives farmers control over how fish are fed, bred, and harvested. This makes it more predictable and sustainable.

Where fish are raised:

🏞️

Fish Ponds

Man-made pools dug in the ground near rivers or the sea

🪤

Fish Cages

Net enclosures floating in rivers, lakes, or coastal areas

🚿

Tanks

Concrete or plastic containers used indoors or outdoors

Branch 2

🎣 Capture Fisheries

Capture Fisheries is the catching of fish and other aquatic organisms from their natural habitat — rivers, lakes, bays, and the open sea.

This is the most traditional form of fishing in the Philippines. Small fisherfolk use simple boats and nets, while large commercial vessels go deep into the sea.

Types of Capture Fisheries:

🏡

Subsistence Fishing

Fishing for personal or family consumption using simple tools

🚤

Municipal Fishing

Fishing within 15 km from the shoreline using small boats

🛳️

Commercial Fishing

Large-scale fishing using big vessels farther out at sea

Common tools used:

  • Fishing nets (lambat) – used to catch large numbers of fish
  • Fishing lines & hooks (kawil) – for simple or sport fishing
  • Fish traps (bungsod) – placed in rivers or shallow coastal areas
  • Spear guns – used in free-diving for reef fish

Day 3 of 5

Fish Processing

How do we make fish last longer?

🐠 Explore & Explain
Branch 3

🏭 What is Fish Processing?

Fish Processing is the branch of Fishery Arts that involves methods of preserving fish and other seafood to extend their shelf life, add value, and make them safe to eat over a longer period of time.

Fresh fish spoil very quickly in warm climates. That is why processing is very important — it lets us enjoy fish even days, weeks, or months after it was caught!

💡 Did you know? The Philippines produces large amounts of dried fish (daing) and salted fish (tuyo) every year. These are important food products for millions of Filipino families.
Processing Methods

Ways to Process Fish

☀️

Drying

Fish is dried under the sun to remove moisture. Example: daing, tuyo

🔥

Smoking

Fish is exposed to smoke from burning wood to add flavor and preserve it. Example: tinapa

🧂

Salting

Fish is covered in salt to draw out moisture and slow spoilage. Example: burong isda

🥫

Canning/Bottling

Fish is sealed in cans or bottles and heat-processed. Example: canned tuna, sardines

❄️

Freezing

Fish is stored at very low temperatures to stop spoilage.

🫙

Fermentation

Fish is mixed with salt and left to ferment. Example: bagoong, patis

Step-by-Step

How to Dry Fish (Daing)

Here is a simple process for making dried fish at home or in a small livelihood project:

  1. Clean the fish – Remove the internal organs (gutting) and wash with clean water
  2. Split the fish – Butterfly cut the fish open so it dries evenly
  3. Soak in salt solution – Mix water and salt, then soak the fish for several hours
  4. Sun-dry the fish – Lay flat on drying racks under direct sunlight for 1–3 days
  5. Store properly – Pack in dry, clean bags or containers away from moisture
⚠️ Safety Reminder: Always keep drying areas clean and protected from insects and rain. Wash hands before and after handling fish.

Fish Marketing & Marine Fisheries

Selling fish and caring for the ocean

🌊 Elaborate
Branch 4

🛒 Fish Marketing

Fish Marketing is the branch of Fishery Arts that involves the buying, selling, and distribution of fish and fish products from producers to consumers.

After fish are raised or caught, they need to reach people who will eat them. This is where marketing comes in. Fish marketing creates jobs and income for many Filipinos.

Ways fish are sold:

🏪

Wet Markets

Traditional public markets where fresh fish are sold daily (palengke)

🚶

Street Vendors

Mobile vendors selling fish by foot or tricycle in neighborhoods

🏬

Supermarkets

Packaged or frozen fish sold in grocery stores and supermarkets

📱

Online Selling

Fish and fish products sold through Facebook, Shopee, or Lazada

People involved in fish marketing:

PersonRole
Fish farmer / FishermanProduces the fish
Dealer / BodegeroBuys in bulk from producers
Retailer / VendorSells directly to consumers
ConsumerBuys and eats the fish
Branch 5

🌊 Marine Fisheries

Marine Fisheries is the branch that focuses on managing and utilizing resources from seas and oceans — including both wild-caught fish and ocean-based aquaculture products.

The Philippines is an archipelago with over 7,600 islands. We are surrounded by some of the richest marine ecosystems in the world. Protecting these resources is very important for future generations.

Marine products and activities:

🌿

Seaweed Farming

Growing seaweed in shallow coastal areas for food and export

🦪

Oyster & Mussel Farming

Growing shellfish attached to bamboo poles or ropes in bays

🐟

Deep-Sea Fishing

Catching tuna and other large fish far out in the ocean

🪸

Coral Reef Management

Protecting coral reefs to maintain healthy fish populations

🌍 Sustainability matters! Overfishing and pollution can destroy marine ecosystems. Responsible fishing means only catching what is needed and protecting breeding grounds.

Summary Review

The Five Branches of Fishery Arts

Let's look back at everything we have learned this week.

🐟

1. Aquaculture

Raising fish and seafood in controlled environments like ponds, cages, and tanks

🎣

2. Capture Fisheries

Catching fish from natural bodies of water — rivers, lakes, bays, and the sea

🏭

3. Fish Processing

Preserving fish through drying, smoking, salting, canning, and fermentation

🛒

4. Fish Marketing

Buying, selling, and distributing fish and fish products from producers to consumers

🌊

5. Marine Fisheries

Managing and using resources from seas and oceans including seaweed and shellfish

Key Generalization: Fishery Arts is an important field of TLE that focuses on raising, catching, processing, and selling fish and other aquatic resources. Its different branches provide food, livelihood, and economic opportunities for many Filipino families and communities.
